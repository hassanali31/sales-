📊 Sales & Customer Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, customer activity, and regional revenue metrics to support business decision-making.

![Dashboard Preview](images/Screenshot.png)

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query
- **Data Modeling & DAX:** Custom Measures & KPIs
- **Data Source:** Excel / CSV

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- **Total Revenue:** Generated $974.78K in sales across 638 active customers.
- **Top Payment Method:** PayPal stands as the most frequently used payment channel.
- **Top Performing Countries:** Zambia and Tajikistan registered the highest sales volumes.
